### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: Two optimization changes tested:
  (1) Huber loss improves robustness to extreme fitness rows vs MSE.
  (2) Per-experiment z-score normalization of targets aids optimization.
- assumption_type: optimization
- why_it_matters: The loss function and target scale directly affect gradient
  dynamics. If fitness data has heavy tails, MSE may overweight outliers.
  If experiment baselines vary widely, z-scoring could simplify learning.

#### T4-A (H-LOSS-01): Loss Family
- mse: standard MSE loss (current default)
- huber_0.5: Huber with delta=0.5 (aggressive outlier clipping)
- huber_1.0: Huber with delta=1.0 (moderate)
- huber_1.5: Huber with delta=1.5 (mild)
- Promotion rule: must improve both co-primary metrics, no regression

#### T4-B (H-TARGET-01): Target Normalization
- raw: train on raw fitness scores (current default)
- zscore: per-experiment z-score (mean/std from train only); evaluate on
  raw-scale after inverse transform
- Promotion rule: raw-scale improvement on both co-primary metrics

### Evidence Summary

#### T4-A (H-LOSS-01)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| mse | 0.5022 ± 0.0013 | 0.2799 ± 0.0008 |
| huber_0.5 | 0.5027 ± 0.0016 | 0.2769 ± 0.0008 |
| huber_1.0 | 0.5034 ± 0.0016 | 0.2781 ± 0.0012 |
| huber_1.5 | 0.5026 ± 0.0017 | 0.2790 ± 0.0010 |

MSE best on RMSE; Huber 0.5 best on MAE (gap 0.003). The metrics
disagree: Huber's outlier clipping improves MAE (median-like) but
regresses RMSE (tail-sensitive). No arm improves both co-primary metrics
simultaneously.
**Decision: no_winner. MSE retained.**

#### T4-B (H-TARGET-01)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| raw | 0.5024 ± 0.0011 | 0.2798 ± 0.0008 |
| zscore | 0.5206 ± 0.0050 | 0.2870 ± 0.0034 |

Raw targets win decisively. Z-scoring degrades both metrics (RMSE +0.018,
MAE +0.007) and increases seed variance substantially (std 0.005 vs 0.001
on RMSE). The inverse transform introduces noise and destroys useful
absolute-scale signal in fitness scores.
**Decision: no_winner. Raw targets retained.**

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **Retain MSE loss and raw fitness targets.** Both
  optimization defaults confirmed as optimal for this data.
- rationale:
  1. T4-A: Huber loss creates a co-primary metric conflict (better MAE,
     worse RMSE). Since the promotion rule requires both metrics to improve,
     MSE is retained. The MAE improvement (0.003) is also sub-threshold.
  2. T4-B: Z-scoring substantially degrades performance and stability.
     The model handles varying experiment scales better via its learned
     representations than via explicit normalization.
- risks_remaining:
  1. The Huber MAE/RMSE conflict suggests mild outlier sensitivity. A
     winsorized loss (clip extreme targets before MSE) could capture
     Huber's MAE benefit without the RMSE regression — deferred.
  2. 8 epochs with fixed LR may underfit the T3 architecture. T4-C
     (hyperparameter sweep with LR schedule, early stopping) should
     address this.
- next_action: Proceed to T4-C (hyperparameter sweep: LR schedule, batch
  size, weight decay, dropout, early stopping) and T4-D (multi-seed CIs).
