### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: A cosine learning rate schedule with longer training
  improves over the fixed 8-epoch constant-LR baseline used through T1-T4-B.
- assumption_type: optimization
- why_it_matters: All prior experiments used a fixed 8-epoch budget with
  constant lr=1e-3 — sensible defaults but never validated. If the T3
  architecture benefits from longer training with decay, the entire pipeline's
  results may have been training-budget-limited.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan

- split_protocol_id: `multi_org_balanced`

#### Arms
- baseline:        8 epochs, lr=1e-3 constant, weight_decay=1e-4
- cosine_16ep:     16 epochs, cosine annealing lr → 0, weight_decay=1e-4
- cosine_32ep:     32 epochs, cosine annealing, patience=5, weight_decay=1e-4
- cosine_32ep_wd:  32 epochs, cosine annealing, patience=5, weight_decay=1e-3

Promotion rule: best on co-primary metrics; no regression vs T3 winner.

### Evidence Summary

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| baseline | 0.5029 ± 0.0001 | 0.2800 ± 0.0007 |
| cosine_16ep | 0.5022 ± 0.0002 | 0.2802 ± 0.0009 |
| cosine_32ep | 0.5014 ± 0.0018 | 0.2795 ± 0.0012 |
| cosine_32ep_wd | 0.5014 ± 0.0012 | **0.2792 ± 0.0001** |

All gaps are sub-threshold:
- cosine_32ep_wd vs baseline: RMSE -0.0015 (7% of threshold), MAE -0.0008 (16%)
- cosine_32ep vs baseline: RMSE -0.0015, MAE -0.0005 (10%)

Bootstrap CIs overlap heavily between arms. Training curves (see
`research_log/figures/tier4_c/02_train_val_curves_per_arm.png`) show val
metrics plateauing around epoch 5-8 while train continues to improve —
classic mild overfitting. Extra training time mostly memorizes more train
data without improving generalization.

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **no_winner. Retain 8-epoch constant-LR baseline.**
- rationale:
  1. Cosine + longer training shows real but sub-threshold improvement.
  2. cosine_32ep_wd has lowest seed variance (std 0.0001 on MAE) — the
     stability advantage is the strongest signal, but RMSE std is unchanged.
  3. 4× training cost for sub-threshold gain is not justified under the
     Pareto-improving promotion rule.
  4. Val plateau suggests the bottleneck is representation capacity, not
     training time. Pursuing this through T5 (embedding) instead.
- risks_remaining:
  1. Even longer training (64-128 epochs) might break the plateau, but the
     observed train-val gap suggests it would mostly overfit further.
  2. We didn't test other LR values (1e-4, 5e-4, 3e-3). T4-C focused on
     schedule + length; a true hyperparameter grid was deferred.
- next_action: Mark T4-C complete. Defer broader hyperparameter sweep
  (LR, batch size, dropout) to post-T5 if a new architecture wins. Proceed
  to T5 (Embedding) to question the frozen ProteomeLM-L assumption.
