### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: Three capacity questions tested jointly:
  (1) Does depth (residual blocks) improve over the locked 1-layer MLP?
  (2) Does wider hidden dimension improve performance at the winning depth?
  (3) Does FiLM gating's near-threshold T2-C signal amplify with more capacity?
- assumption_type: architecture
- why_it_matters: The locked T2 fusion (1-layer, 256-dim) may under-represent
  gene×condition interactions. Additional capacity could capture more complex
  conditional essentiality patterns.

#### T3-B (H-CAP-02): Width Sweep (at T3-A winning depth)

### Evidence Summary

#### T3-A (H-CAP-01)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1_layer | 0.5152 ± 0.0016 | 0.2958 ± 0.0013 |
| 2_layer | 0.5059 ± 0.0007 | 0.2821 ± 0.0008 |
| 4_layer | 0.5043 ± 0.0005 | 0.2804 ± 0.0013 |

1→2 layer RMSE gap = 0.0093 (>>threshold), MAE gap = 0.0137 (>>threshold).
2→4 layer RMSE gap = 0.0016, MAE gap = 0.0017 (both sub-threshold).
Bootstrap CIs disjoint between 1-layer and 2-layer/4-layer; overlapping
between 2-layer and 4-layer. Deeper models also show lower seed variance.
**Decision: parsimony selects 2_layer.** The first residual block provides
the large gain; additional blocks show diminishing returns.

#### T3-B (H-CAP-02)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| width_128 | 0.5080 ± 0.0019 | 0.2838 ± 0.0005 |
| width_256 | 0.5059 ± 0.0007 | 0.2821 ± 0.0008 |
| width_512 | 0.5028 ± 0.0003 | 0.2799 ± 0.0007 |
| width_1024 | 0.5013 ± 0.0017 | 0.2796 ± 0.0020 |

Monotonic improvement from narrow to wide. Bootstrap CIs disjoint between
each step except 512 vs 1024 on MAE (slight overlap). Width 1024 has the
best raw mean but substantially higher seed variance (std 0.0017 vs 0.0003
for 512). **Decision: promote width_512.** Nearly matches 1024 on mean
performance with much better seed stability.

#### T3-D (H-CAP-03)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| concat | 0.5057 ± 0.0009 | 0.2816 ± 0.0003 |
| film_gated | 0.5074 ± 0.0004 | 0.2831 ± 0.0012 |

FiLM is worse than concat at 2-layer depth (RMSE +0.0016, MAE +0.0015).
The T2-C near-threshold signal did not amplify — it reversed. The residual
block captures the interactions FiLM was providing at 1 layer, making
FiLM's explicit modulation redundant.
**Decision: no_winner. Concat retained.**

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **Lock 2-layer ResidualMLP with hidden_dim=512.**
  Architecture: cat(gene[1152], chem[425]) → Linear(1577, 512) → ReLU →
  Dropout(0.1) → ResBlock(512) → Linear(512, 1). ResBlock = Linear(512, 512) →
  ReLU → Dropout(0.1), with additive skip connection.
- rationale:
  1. T3-A proves depth helps substantially (1→2 layer RMSE gap 0.009) with
     diminishing returns beyond 2 layers.
  2. T3-B proves wider hidden layers improve performance monotonically; 512
     selected over 1024 for seed stability at near-identical mean metrics.
  3. T3-D rules out FiLM — the near-threshold T2-C signal reverses at depth,
     confirming the residual block subsumes FiLM's benefit.
  4. T3-C (embedding fine-tune) not triggered: T3-A/B did not plateau —
     both depth and width provided clear gains.
- risks_remaining:
  1. The 1577→512 projection is still a 67% compression. A skip connection
     from input to output (or a wider first layer) could help if the
     bottleneck is information loss at the projection.
  2. 8 epochs may underfit deeper/wider models. T4's LR schedule and
     early-stopping experiments will address this.
- next_action: Emit `data_contract/architecture_winner.yaml`. Proceed to T4
  (optimization: loss family, target normalization, hyperparameter sweep).
