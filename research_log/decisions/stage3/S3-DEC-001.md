### Homology-aware evaluation (pre-registered design)

**Context:** S1 triggered `H-HOMO-01` (`candidate_protocols.yaml`: `h_homo_01_triggered: true`). Figure 18 (embedding cosine to nearest train gene, per protocol) shows protocol-dependent homology / neighborhood ease; figure 19 shows organism-level similarity mass. REFACTORPLAN requires a homology-aware diagnostic when this trigger fires.

**Ratified policy (carries into tier reports and promotion wording):**

1. **Primary diagnostic — similarity-bin stratification**  
   On the full scored val set, report metrics **stratified by val-gene embedding cosine similarity** to the nearest train gene (same construction as S1 figure 18: max cosine over train organisms for each val gene). Tier reports and stage gates **always include similarity-bin panels** (e.g. low / medium / high bins) so optimistic headline metrics cannot hide a “high-similarity memorization” regime.

2. **Secondary diagnostic — homology masking**  
   Additionally report a **single bottom-line “homology-clean” RMSE** (and MAE, co-primary with RMSE per L4) on the subset of val rows whose val `gene_key` has **nearest-train cosine strictly below** the high-similarity cutoff. This subset is the conservative read for **tier promotion claims** that assert generalization beyond sequence-neighborhood transfer.

3. **Cutoff**  
   **High-similarity threshold = 0.85** (cosine). Same scale as S1 diagnostics (`similarity_threshold: 0.85` in `candidate_protocols.yaml` homology summaries and figure 18 narrative). Rationale: anchored to the S1 characterization artifact; formal ratification is this entry.

**Evidence attachment for the cutoff:** `research_log/figures/stage1/18_embedding_cosine_to_nearest_train_per_protocol.csv` (and the paired figure when regenerated).

**Implementation note:** Exact bin edges for the primary diagnostic (e.g. tertiles vs fixed `[0,0.5),[0.5,0.85),[0.85,1]`) may be specified in `data_contract/splits/diagnostic_protocols.yaml` or the S3 tier report when the runner is implemented, without changing the above two-track requirement.

### Evidence Summary

#### Override note (rule clarification)

The literal S3 overlap band (`val/test chemistry seen-rate in 30–80%`) is
degenerate on this dataset at the Canonical_ID axis. S1 candidate protocols have
val Canonical_ID seen-rate in [0.947, 1.000], so none fall in 30–80%.
To preserve transparent protocol selection, S3 used `s3_v1_power_driven`:

1. Eligibility filter:
   - Spearman role = primary
   - bootstrap `n_genes_used >= 200`
   - `val_rows >= m * 200` (m from eval policy)
   - val Canonical_ID seen-rate `>= 0.90`
2. Ranking:
   - prefer protocols where embedding-NN beats global mean on RMSE
   - tiebreak by higher `n_genes_used`
   - then by higher `val_rows`
