### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: H-ENC-01 ("decomposed chemistry encoding beats coarse
  medium-name-only encoding") is supported when the protocol actually tests
  the property — specifically, when val medium names are not in train.
- assumption_type: representation
- why_it_matters: T1-DEC-001 concluded `no_winner` on `multi_org_balanced` but
  flagged that the protocol does not stress-test the encoder distinction
  because `val_seen_rate=1.0` at the media-name level. T1-A.2 runs the same
  comparison on `largest_by_rows` (val_seen_rate=0%) — the proper test.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan
- comparison: same 2 arms × 3 seeds as T1-A, only the split protocol changes.
  - Protocol: `largest_by_rows` (val=Btheta, test=DvH).
- fixed_controls (all identical to T1-A):
  - feature_contract_id: `de21504134c84a6c`
  - quality_policy_id: `weighted_full`
  - seeds: {0, 1, 2}, epochs: 8
  - model: shallow concat-linear MLP, hidden=256, dropout=0.1
  - lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-4, batch_size=8192
- metrics_primary: [rmse, mae] (S2 co-primaries)
- promotion_rule:
  - Use the S2-locked thresholds for `largest_by_rows` (RMSE 0.005, MAE 0.005
    floors; no NN advantage means best_non_global = global itself).
  - Inter-arm winner if RMSE AND MAE gaps both exceed threshold in the same
    direction.

### Evidence Summary

#### Per-arm headline metrics

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std, 3 seeds) | MAE |
|---|---:|---:|
| multihot_canonical_id | 0.5897 ± 0.0109 | 0.3675 ± 0.0036 |
| media_id | **3.8451 ± 1.2961** | **2.6627 ± 1.0484** |
| **gap (media_id − multihot)** | **+3.2554** | **+2.2952** |

The inter-arm gap is **650× the RMSE threshold** and **460× the MAE
threshold**. Statistically unambiguous on both co-primaries.

#### Bootstrap CIs (seed 0)

| Arm | RMSE 95% CI | MAE 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| multihot_canonical_id | [0.5987, 0.6021] | [0.3709, 0.3722] |
| media_id | [3.3626, 3.3727] | [2.3219, 2.3284] |

Non-overlapping by an enormous margin.

#### Cross-seed variance

| Arm | RMSE std across seeds | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| multihot | 0.011 | stable — chemistry signal is deterministic per seed |
| media_id | **1.30** | UNK embedding has no training signal; drifts to random init each seed |

Cross-seed std of 1.30 for media_id is **diagnostic evidence** of the
predicted failure mode: every val row hits a single UNK token that
never received gradient updates, so the prediction is essentially
"untrained-embedding × MLP".

#### Homology-bin breakdown (seed 0)

| Bin (cosine) | n_rows | multihot RMSE | media_id RMSE | gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [0.00, 0.50) | 1,060,317 | 0.580 | 3.335 | +2.76 |
| [0.50, 0.70) | 959,112 | 0.622 | 3.448 | +2.83 |
| [0.70, 0.85) | 74,736 | 0.607 | 2.881 | +2.27 |
| [0.85, 1.01) | ~9,861 | 0.526 | 2.158 | +1.63 |

Multihot is stable across all bins. Media_id is catastrophic across all
bins, somewhat improving for high-similarity genes (gene embedding alone
helps a bit). **The crossover seen in T1-A is absent here** — that
crossover was an artifact of the easy protocol.

#### Open caveat: neither arm beats global mean

- global_train_mean RMSE on `largest_by_rows`: **0.5668** (best non-global
  per S2-DEC-001)
- multihot RMSE: 0.5897 (0.023 *worse*)
- media_id RMSE: 3.8451 (3.28 *worse*)

The shallow concat-linear MLP cannot extract real signal from Btheta even
with good chemistry features. This is a **protocol-difficulty finding**,
not an encoder finding. H-BASE-01 (revised) on `largest_by_rows` fails for
both arms. **This does not invalidate T1-DEC-002's inter-arm conclusion**
— H-ENC-01 is about which encoder is better, not whether either encoder
beats global mean.

### Decision

- decision_outcome: **promote_multihot_canonical_id** (inter-arm winner by
  the largest possible margin).
- **H-ENC-01 supported.** No further stress test required. The
  `unresolved_followup` flag in `data_contract/representation_winner.yaml`
  is now removed.
- **`multi_org_balanced` remains the primary promotion protocol** for
  T1-B through T1-E and all downstream tiers. **`largest_by_rows` is
  promoted to mandatory diagnostic** — every Tier-1+ promotion report
  must include the H-ENC-01 stress-test outcome on largest_by_rows.

- rationale:
  1. H-ENC-01 was the foundational T1 hypothesis. T1-A produced an
     ambiguous (`no_winner`) result on the locked protocol but the
     ambiguity was a property of the protocol, not the encoders.
  2. T1-A.2 used the protocol the original H-ENC-01 framing
     anticipated (val media unseen in train). The result is the
     largest gap we will see in any T1 experiment (650× threshold)
     and points unambiguously at multihot.
  3. The structural failure mode of media_id (UNK-only val) is
     exactly the failure that motivated chemistry decomposition in
     the first place. Confirming it empirically closes the H-ENC-01
     question.

- risks_remaining:
  1. **Btheta is hard for the locked model class.** Neither arm beats
     global mean. T2 fusion / T3 capacity experiments may close this
     gap; if they don't, the project's "predict for held-out organisms
     with unseen media chemistry" claim has a sharp limit. Filed as
     **OPEN: protocol-difficulty for hardest val orgs**.
  2. **largest_by_rows mandatory-diagnostic policy** adds compute to
     every future tier comparison (~50 min per T1 experiment now adds
     ~50 min for the largest_by_rows arm). Acceptable cost for the
     epistemic safety it provides.
