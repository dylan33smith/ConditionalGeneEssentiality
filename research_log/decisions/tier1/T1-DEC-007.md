### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: Explicit decomposition-mode indicators
  (physical/mix/extract/stressor proportion features) mitigate
  over-coupling between chemistry identity and decomposition source,
  improving conditional fitness prediction.
- assumption_type: representation
- why_it_matters: Extract-derived chemistry (yeast extract, tryptone) lists
  inferred component amino acids/vitamins that can drive spurious overlap
  with defined media. If the model can distinguish "these chemicals were
  inferred from an extract" vs "these chemicals were directly measured,"
  it may avoid over-coupling.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan
- comparison: 2 arms × 3 seeds.
  - **chemistry_only**: 425-dim binary multihot (T1-DEC-004 winner)
  - **chemistry_plus_mode_flags**: 425-dim multihot + 4 mode-proportion
    features (physical, mix, extract, stressor — per-experiment fractions
    computed from canonical_id dominant_mode lookup)
- fixed_controls:
  - split_protocol_id: `multi_org_balanced`
  - feature_contract_id: `de21504134c84a6c`
  - quality_policy_id: `weighted_full` + full org pool
  - condition_encoder: `multihot_canonical_id` binary (T1-DEC-004)
  - seeds: {0, 1, 2}, epochs: 8
  - model: shallow concat-linear MLP, hidden=256, dropout=0.1
  - lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-4, batch_size=8192
- metrics_primary: [rmse, mae]
- promotion_rule:
  - chemistry_plus_mode_flags must beat chemistry_only on both RMSE AND MAE
    above the S2-locked thresholds (RMSE: 0.0212, MAE: 0.005).

### Evidence Summary

#### Per-arm metrics (mean ± std, 3 seeds)

| Arm | RMSE | MAE |
|---|---:|---:|
| chemistry_only | 0.5151 ± 0.0018 | 0.2959 ± 0.0013 |
| chemistry_plus_mode_flags | 0.5149 ± 0.0025 | 0.2956 ± 0.0023 |

#### Gaps

| Metric | Gap (chem − mode) | Threshold | Above threshold? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| RMSE | 0.0003 | 0.0212 | No (1.2% of threshold) |
| MAE | 0.0003 | 0.005 | No (5.5% of threshold) |

#### Bootstrap CIs (seed 0, 1000 row-resamples)

| Arm | RMSE 95% CI | MAE 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| chemistry_only | [0.5117, 0.5153] | [0.2950, 0.2960] |
| chemistry_plus_mode_flags | [0.5113, 0.5150] | [0.2936, 0.2947] |

- RMSE: CIs overlap almost entirely.
- MAE: CIs overlap. Seed 0 of mode_flags looks better but seeds 1-2 wash it out.

#### Mode proportion summary
Mean proportions across experiments: physical=0.31, mix=0.39, extract=0.25,
stressor=0.03. The 4 proportions sum to 1.0 per experiment (linearly dependent).
in_silico is zero everywhere (omitted). Mode_flags arm has *higher* seed variance
than baseline (RMSE std 0.0025 vs 0.0018), suggesting the extra features add
noise rather than signal at this capacity.

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **no_winner — default to chemistry_only.** Mode flags are
  not promoted.
- rationale:
  1. Both gaps are negligible (<2% of threshold on RMSE, <6% on MAE).
  2. The mode_flags arm has higher seed variance, not lower — no
     stabilization benefit (unlike T1-D's metadata).
  3. The 4 mode proportions are derivable from the chemistry vector itself
     (they're aggregates of the canonical_id dominant_mode). The model can
     in principle learn this mapping internally; the explicit features
     don't help it at shallow capacity.
- risks_remaining:
  1. At deeper capacity (T3), the model may still struggle to disentangle
     extract-driven overlap from genuine chemistry signal. But the
     T1-level evidence says explicit mode flags don't help.
  2. A per-slot mode indicator (tagging each canonical_id column with its
     mode) rather than per-experiment proportions might be more informative,
     but that changes the architecture (input masking, not concatenation)
     and belongs in T2 if revisited.
- next_action: Mode flags not included in the T1 representation winner.
  T1 is now complete. Proceed to T2 (fusion topology).
