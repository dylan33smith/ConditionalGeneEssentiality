### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: `min_org_support_rows = 10,000` is an acceptable
  organism-eligibility threshold for candidate val/test selection in
  `data_contract/splits/candidate_protocols.yaml`.
- assumption_type: split
- why_it_matters: the threshold determines which organisms are *considered* by
  the candidate generator. Too low → unstable val metrics from underpowered
  organisms. Too high → fewer candidates and risk of excluding scientifically
  interesting low-row organisms.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan
- comparison: 10k vs 50k threshold, evaluated against (a) the actually-selected
  val orgs in the four committed candidates and (b) the rows-per-organism
  distribution from `research_log/figures/stage1/_data/rows_per_organism.csv`.
- success criteria:
  - the chosen threshold does not exclude any organism that ended up as a
    val/test pick under either threshold setting,
  - the chosen threshold yields enough eligible organisms to populate the
    candidate strategies in `src/experiments/stage1/candidates.py`,
  - the chosen threshold is documented and locked.

### Evidence Summary

Eligibility under each threshold (from `_data/rows_per_organism.csv`, 48 orgs total):
- **10k threshold:** ~40 of 48 organisms eligible (8 small organisms excluded).
- **50k threshold:** ~30 of 48 organisms eligible (18 organisms excluded).

Selected val/test organisms in the four committed candidates:
- `largest_by_rows`: val=Btheta (2,104,545 rows), test=DvH (2,074,937)
- `high_overlap_easy`: val=[Putida, psRCH2] (each ~1M+), test=pseudo3_N2E3
- `low_overlap_stress`: val=SynE (244,971), test=Magneto (115,956)
- `multi_org_balanced`: val=[pseudo3_N2E3, BFirm, ANA3, SyringaeB728a_mexBdelta]
  (~2.2M aggregate), test=PV4 (~481k)

**Every selected val/test org has at least 100k rows.** All would have remained
eligible at either threshold. The 10k vs 50k distinction matters only for which
organisms were *eligible* during candidate generation, not which ones got picked.

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **lock `min_org_support_rows = 10,000`.**
- rationale: the 10k threshold is the value used at S1 generation time; the
  candidates committed to `data_contract/splits/candidate_protocols.yaml` were
  produced under it. All actually-selected val/test orgs comfortably exceed
  50k anyway, so bumping to 50k would invalidate the S1 candidate file
  without changing any picked candidate. No scientific reason to re-run.
- risks_remaining:
  - If S3 wants to add a new candidate protocol that uses a small (<50k)
    organism, the 10k threshold permits it. We accept the tradeoff:
    interpretive caveat reported alongside any small-org candidate's metrics.
- next_action: lock the threshold; document in CLAUDE.md if needed (already
  recorded in `data_contract/splits/candidate_protocols.yaml: min_org_support_rows: 10000`).
