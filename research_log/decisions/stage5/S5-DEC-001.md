### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: A weighted-full row policy retains useful supervision and improves RMSE+MAE over a strict hard-filter slice, under the locked split and feature contract.
- assumption_type: optimization
- why_it_matters: If row-quality policy is left as a sweep variable in T1, representation comparisons are confounded by data-mass effects rather than encoder quality.

### H-POLICY-02 handling
- decision: skipped
- rationale: S1 already applied support curation (`min_org_support_rows=50,000`); no additional curated-vs-full pool arm run in S5.

### Decision
- decision_outcome: promote `weighted_full` as locked S5 row-quality policy
- rationale: `weighted_full` wins both co-primary metrics and passes additive baseline gate, while preserving higher effective training mass and better secondary Spearman behavior.
- risks_remaining:
  - S5 model is a policy vehicle, not a promoted architecture.
  - Concentration units are still unresolved (`units_1..units_4` not normalized in S4), so magnitude-sensitive conclusions remain limited.
  - Constant-input Spearman warnings occur for some low-variance genes; these rows are excluded from per-gene Spearman aggregation.
- next_action: proceed to T1 with `quality_policy.yaml` frozen (`row_quality_policy=weighted_full`, `organism_pool=full`).
