### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: The fusion topology (how gene and condition vectors
  combine) materially affects prediction quality. Three hypotheses tested:
  (1) nonlinear head vs linear, (2) early concat vs two-tower, (3) ungated
  vs FiLM conditioning.
- assumption_type: fusion
- why_it_matters: The gene×condition interaction is the core modeling
  challenge. The right fusion topology determines whether the model can
  capture conditional essentiality patterns vs just learning marginal
  effects.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan

All three experiments share:
- split_protocol_id: `multi_org_balanced`
- feature_contract_id: `de21504134c84a6c`
- quality_policy_id: `weighted_full` + full org pool
- T1 representation: 425-dim binary multihot (T1-DEC-004)
- seeds: {0, 1, 2}, epochs: 8
- lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-4, batch_size=8192
- metrics_primary: [rmse, mae]
- S2-locked thresholds: RMSE=0.0212, MAE=0.005

#### T2-A (H-FUSE-01): Linear vs Shallow MLP
- linear_head: cat(gene[1152], chem[425]) → Linear(1577, 1)
- shallow_mlp: cat(gene, chem) → Linear(1577, 256) → ReLU → Dropout(0.1) → Linear(256, 1)

#### T2-B (H-FUSE-02): Early Concat vs Two-Tower
- early_concat: cat(gene, chem) → MLP(256) → 1 (same as shallow_mlp)
- two_tower: gene → MLP(128); chem → MLP(128); cat → Linear → 1

#### T2-C (H-FUSE-03): Ungated vs FiLM
- ungated: cat(gene, chem) → MLP(256) → 1 (same as shallow_mlp)
- film_gated: chem → (gamma, beta); gene * (1+gamma) + beta → MLP(256) → 1

### Evidence Summary

#### T2-A (H-FUSE-01)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| linear_head | 0.6100 ± 0.0002 | 0.3405 ± 0.0005 |
| shallow_mlp | 0.5146 ± 0.0025 | 0.2951 ± 0.0021 |

RMSE gap = 0.0954 (>>threshold), MAE gap = 0.0454 (>>threshold).
Bootstrap CIs completely disjoint on both metrics.
**Decision: promote_shallow_mlp.** The nonlinearity is essential.

#### T2-B (H-FUSE-02)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| early_concat | 0.5140 ± 0.0034 | 0.2947 ± 0.0027 |
| two_tower | 0.5627 ± 0.0006 | 0.3133 ± 0.0010 |

RMSE gap = -0.0487 (early_concat better, >>threshold), MAE gap = -0.0185
(>>threshold). Bootstrap CIs completely disjoint.
**Decision: promote_early_concat.** Separating towers prevents early
gene×condition interaction, degrading performance substantially.

#### T2-C (H-FUSE-03)

| Arm | RMSE (mean ± std) | MAE (mean ± std) |
|---|---:|---:|
| ungated | 0.5153 ± 0.0016 | 0.2951 ± 0.0021 |
| film_gated | 0.5121 ± 0.0018 | 0.2911 ± 0.0004 |

RMSE gap = 0.0032 (15% of threshold), MAE gap = 0.0040 (80% of threshold).
Bootstrap CIs disjoint on both RMSE and MAE — FiLM is statistically better
but sub-threshold.
**Decision: no_winner.** FiLM shows the strongest sub-threshold signal in
the entire T1-T2 pipeline. The MAE gap (0.004) nearly clears the bar.

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **Lock early-concat shallow MLP as fusion winner.**
  Architecture: cat(gene[1152], chem[425]) → Linear(1577, 256) → ReLU →
  Dropout(0.1) → Linear(256, 1). This is identical to the T1 vehicle.
- rationale:
  1. T2-A proves nonlinearity is essential (RMSE gap 0.095).
  2. T2-B proves early fusion is essential (RMSE gap 0.049).
  3. T2-C shows FiLM is promising but sub-threshold under the co-primary
     rule. Both metrics favor FiLM, bootstrap CIs are disjoint, but gaps
     don't exceed the S2-locked thresholds.
  4. The winning topology (early-concat MLP) is the simplest option that
     clears the promotion bar, consistent with the plan's parsimony bias.
- risks_remaining:
  1. FiLM's near-threshold MAE signal (0.004 vs 0.005 threshold) may
     amplify at deeper capacity. T3 should consider testing FiLM + depth
     as a combined ablation if the locked fusion underperforms.
  2. The two-tower failure may be partially explained by the 128-dim
     bottleneck compressing the 1152-dim gene embeddings. A wider gene
     tower could change the result, but that's a capacity question (T3).
- next_action: Emit `data_contract/fusion_winner.yaml`. Proceed to T3
  (capacity: depth, residuals, efficiency frontier).
