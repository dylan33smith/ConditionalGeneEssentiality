### Decision
- decision_outcome: **approved — S0 acceptance gate passed.**
- rationale:
  1. Smoke pipeline runs end-to-end: checksums → v4 verification → split →
     baselines → manifest, in ~17s after first-run cache warmup.
  2. Manifest validates against `run_manifest_v1.schema.json`.
  3. Reproducibility verified by two independent reruns at same seed
     producing identical smoke digests.
  4. `data_contract/v4_schema_verification.json` emitted with the
     5 distinct `Decomposition_type` values (`direct`, `extract`, `mix`,
     `salt`, `unrecoverable`) — useful input for S4's `representation_mode`
     mapping.
  5. Test suite is green and the S0-specific reproducibility tests
     (`test_s0_smoke_reproducibility.py`) all pass.
- risks_remaining:
  - **Additive baseline is overfit at smoke scale.** At 64k train rows with
    thousands of gene_keys and thousands of expNames, fitted gene/condition
    effects are noisy and worse than the global mean predictor on val.
    Expected to reverse at full S2 scale (millions of train rows).
    The `# TODO(perf)` in `src/evaluation/additive_baseline.py` flags the
    related vectorization need before S2.
  - The S0 smoke `s0_smoke_random_80_20` protocol is a wiring proof, NOT a
    promotion-eligible split. Locked split comes from S3.
  - The legacy media parquets (`media_master.parquet`, `media_components_long.parquet`)
    remain on disk as deprecated artifacts. S1 will read v4 directly per
    META-DEC-001 risk note.
- next_action: implement S1 — Data Characterization. Emits
  `data_contract/splits/candidate_protocols.yaml` and
  `research_log/tier_reports/s1_data_characterization.md` per REFACTORPLAN §7 S1.
