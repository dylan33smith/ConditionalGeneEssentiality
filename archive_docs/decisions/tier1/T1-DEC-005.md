### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: Explicit UNK + mask indicators improve robustness on
  novel conditions vs zero-fill.
- assumption_type: representation
- why_it_matters: If val/test conditions contain chemistry absent from the
  training vocab, the model's handling of those unknowns (zero-fill vs
  explicit token + mask) could materially affect metrics.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan
- comparison: N/A — experiment skipped.
- rationale_for_skip: The locked feature contract (artifact `de21504134c84a6c`)
  and locked split protocol (`multi_org_balanced`) produce **zero chemistry
  unknowns** in the eval set:
  - `unknown_category_rates.chemistry_eval_rows.fraction_canonical_id_not_in_train_vocab`: **0.0**
  - `unknown_category_rates.metadata_eval.oxygen`: **0.0**
  - `unknown_category_rates.metadata_eval.liquid_state`: **0.0**
  - `unknown_category_rates.metadata_eval.experiment_group`: **0.33%** (metadata only, not chemistry)

  With no chemistry prevalence trimming (threshold = 0.0) and no canonical IDs
  appearing in eval but absent from train, both arms of H-ENC-03 (zero-fill vs
  explicit-UNK+mask) produce **identical chemistry vectors** for every eval row.
  The experiment cannot distinguish the two treatments and would return a
  statistical tie by construction.

  The 0.33% unknown rate on `experiment_group` is a metadata categorical, not
  a chemistry slot, and will be tested as part of T1-D (metadata bundle) rather
  than T1-C.

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **skip T1-C (H-ENC-03).** No experiment run.
- rationale:
  1. Zero chemistry unknown rate on the locked split + vocab means both arms
     are functionally identical — the experiment has no statistical power to
     detect a difference.
  2. The UNK handling infrastructure (`unk_policy` in `feature_contract.yaml`,
     `<UNK>` token at index 0) remains in the codebase and is available if
     conditions change.
  3. No locked decision is affected — T1-A through T1-B.3 all used the
     multihot encoder which already handles the zero-unknown case correctly.
- risks_remaining:
  1. If a future change introduces prevalence trimming (bucketing rare
     canonical IDs to UNK), H-ENC-03 becomes testable and should be revisited.
  2. If a Canonical_ID-level holdout split is implemented (REFACTORPLAN §12
     deferred experiments), the unknown rate will be nonzero by design and
     UNK handling will matter.
- next_action: Proceed to T1-D (H-ENC-04, metadata bundle test). H-ENC-03
  is deferred, not rejected — reactivate if the unknown rate becomes nonzero.
