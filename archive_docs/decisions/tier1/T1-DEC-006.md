### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: Adding experiment metadata (oxygen, growth phase,
  liquid state, temperature, pH, shaking RPM) to the chemistry-only
  condition vector improves conditional fitness prediction.
- assumption_type: representation
- why_it_matters: Two experiments can share the same medium yet differ in
  environmental conditions (aerobic vs anaerobic, temperature, etc.). If
  these variables independently affect gene fitness, a chemistry-only model
  conflates them.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan
- comparison: 2 arms × 3 seeds.
  - **chemistry_only**: 425-dim binary multihot (T1-DEC-004 winner)
  - **chemistry_plus_metadata**: 425-dim multihot + 55 metadata features
    (49 one-hot categoricals + 6 z-scored numerics with finite indicators)
- metadata_features:
  - oxygen_idx: 4-dim one-hot (train-only vocab)
  - experiment_group_idx: 42-dim one-hot (train-only vocab, 0.33% eval unknown rate)
  - liquid_state_idx: 3-dim one-hot (train-only vocab)
  - temperature_c_z + temperature_c_is_finite (97% finite)
  - pH_z + pH_is_finite (47% finite)
  - shaking_rpm_z + shaking_rpm_is_finite (71% finite)
- fixed_controls:
  - split_protocol_id: `multi_org_balanced`
  - feature_contract_id: `de21504134c84a6c`
  - quality_policy_id: `weighted_full` + full org pool
  - condition_encoder: `multihot_canonical_id` binary (T1-DEC-004)
  - seeds: {0, 1, 2}, epochs: 8
  - model: shallow concat-linear MLP, hidden=256, dropout=0.1
  - lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-4, batch_size=8192
- metrics_primary: [rmse, mae]
- promotion_rule:
  - chemistry_plus_metadata must beat chemistry_only on both RMSE AND MAE
    above the S2-locked thresholds (RMSE: 0.0212, MAE: 0.005).

### Evidence Summary

#### Per-arm metrics (mean ± std, 3 seeds)

| Arm | RMSE | MAE |
|---|---:|---:|
| chemistry_only | 0.5153 ± 0.0016 | 0.2958 ± 0.0013 |
| chemistry_plus_metadata | 0.5126 ± 0.0002 | 0.2940 ± 0.0005 |

#### Gaps

| Metric | Gap (chem − meta) | Threshold | Above threshold? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| RMSE | 0.0027 | 0.0212 | No (12.5% of threshold) |
| MAE | 0.0018 | 0.005 | No (36% of threshold) |

#### Bootstrap CIs (seed 0, 1000 row-resamples)

| Arm | RMSE 95% CI | MAE 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| chemistry_only | [0.5121, 0.5156] | [0.2947, 0.2958] |
| chemistry_plus_metadata | [0.5109, 0.5145] | [0.2929, 0.2939] |

- RMSE: CIs overlap substantially.
- MAE: CIs are disjoint — metadata is statistically better on MAE.

#### Notable secondary observation

The metadata arm has dramatically tighter seed variance (RMSE std 0.0002
vs 0.0016, MAE std 0.0005 vs 0.0013). Adding metadata stabilizes the model
even though it doesn't clear the promotion bar.

### Decision
- decision_outcome: **no_winner — default to chemistry_only.** Metadata is
  not promoted as part of the T1 representation.
- rationale:
  1. Both RMSE and MAE gaps are well below the S2-locked thresholds.
  2. The co-primary rule requires both metrics to exceed threshold in the
     same direction. MAE alone is insufficient.
  3. The directional improvement and seed stabilization are real but do not
     meet the pre-registered bar.
- risks_remaining:
  1. Metadata's stabilization effect may become load-bearing at higher model
     capacity (T3). If T2/T3 see seed instability, metadata should be
     revisited as a regularization mechanism.
  2. The disjoint MAE CIs suggest a real (small) signal that the shallow
     T1 vehicle may underestimate. Deeper models in T2+ could amplify it.
  3. pH has 47% missing rate — a dedicated imputation strategy in a later
     tier could unlock more signal.
- next_action: Metadata not included in the T1 representation winner.
  Data preserved at `artifacts/runs/t1d/` for future reference.
