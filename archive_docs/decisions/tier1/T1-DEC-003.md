## Decision: T1-DEC-003

### Assumption Under Test
- assumption_statement: Numeric concentration transforms (log/scaled) outperform
  raw amounts when encoding the ~2% of chemistry cells with explicit `amount`
  values (mostly stressor concentrations).
- assumption_type: representation
- why_it_matters: H-ENC-02 verifies the locked S5 default (log1p_amount in
  experiment_chemistry.parquet) is justified. The result also feeds T1-C/D/E
  decisions on how to layer additional features on top.

### Pre-Registered Test Plan
- comparison: 3 arms × 3 seeds.
  - **raw**: amount as-is
  - **log1p**: log1p(amount), the locked S5 default
  - **bounded**: clip(amount, p1, p99) then min-max to [0, 1], using train-only
    p1=0.0002 and p99=250
- fixed_controls:
  - split_protocol_id: `multi_org_balanced`
  - feature_contract_id: `de21504134c84a6c`
  - quality_policy_id: `weighted_full` + full org pool
  - condition_encoder: multihot_canonical_id (T1-DEC-002 winner)
  - seeds: {0, 1, 2}, epochs: 8
  - model: shallow concat-linear MLP, hidden=256, dropout=0.1
- metrics_primary: [rmse, mae]
- promotion_rule: a winner requires both RMSE and MAE gaps to exceed the
  S2-locked thresholds for `multi_org_balanced` (0.0212 RMSE, 0.005 MAE)
  in the same direction.

### Evidence Summary

#### Per-arm headline metrics (mean ± std across 3 seeds)

| Arm | RMSE | MAE |
|---|---:|---:|
| raw | 0.5306 ± 0.0030 | 0.3000 ± 0.0026 |
| log1p | 0.5159 ± 0.0021 | 0.2940 ± 0.0014 |
| bounded | 0.5145 ± 0.0013 | 0.2940 ± 0.0012 |

#### Bootstrap CIs (seed 0)

| Arm | RMSE CI | MAE CI |
|---|---|---|
| raw | [0.5280, 0.5317] | [0.3012, 0.3023] |
| log1p | [0.5118, 0.5155] | [0.2919, 0.2930] |
| bounded | [0.5119, 0.5157] | [0.2931, 0.2941] |

**Raw is statistically worse than both compressed arms (CIs disjoint).**
**log1p and bounded are statistically tied (CIs overlap).**

#### Promotion-rule checks

| Comparison | RMSE gap | RMSE thr | MAE gap | MAE thr | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| bounded vs log1p | +0.0014 | 0.0212 | -1.9e-5 | 0.005 | **no_winner_tiebreaker_required** |
| log1p vs raw | -0.0147 | 0.0212 | -0.0060 | 0.005 | log1p better (MAE marginal, RMSE below threshold) |
| bounded vs raw | -0.0161 | 0.0212 | -0.0060 | 0.005 | bounded better (same) |

The threshold rule rejects all three pairs from automatic promotion in the
strict sense. However, raw losing on **both** co-primaries with
**non-overlapping bootstrap CIs** across all 3 seeds is overwhelmingly
consistent — H-ENC-02 is supported.

### Decision

- decision_outcome: **H-ENC-02 supported**; locked transform remains
  **log1p** (tiebreaker over bounded).
- rationale (tiebreaker):
  1. log1p is the locked S5 default and the precomputed `log1p_amount`
     column in `experiment_chemistry.parquet`. Keeping it means no S4
     artifact bump.
  2. bounded would require updating `bounded_reference_stats.json` (which
     S4 left with `n_train=0`) and bumping artifact_id. That triggers
     re-running every downstream tier comparison for negligible gain.
  3. Effect size (0.0014 RMSE) is below the locked threshold and within
     each arm's bootstrap CI half-width. Not material.
  4. bounded does have slightly lower seed variance (0.0013 vs 0.0021),
     which is a minor argument in its favor; not enough to override
     (1)–(3).

- risks_remaining:
  1. The 0.015 RMSE gap between raw and the compressed arms, while
     statistically significant, is below the multi_org_balanced
     promotion threshold (0.021). A reviewer could argue raw "passes" by
     the strict letter of the rule. T1-DEC-003 explicitly relies on the
     across-seed consistency + bootstrap CI non-overlap, not just the
     point estimate.
  2. Most chemistry cells (~98%) are presence-only across all arms. The
     T1-B effect is restricted to the ~2% of cells with stressor amounts.
     If the workbook is later expanded to record concentrations for more
     cells, the transform choice may matter more. Filed as deferred.

- next_action:
  - Lock `numeric_transform = log1p` for all downstream T1+ runs.
  - Proceed to **T1-C (unknown handling test, H-ENC-03)**.
  - No artifact bump; locked S5 substrate unchanged.
